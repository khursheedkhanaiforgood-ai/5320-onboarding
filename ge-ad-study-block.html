Why docs alone didn't work yesterday

Both GE and AirDefense are micro-apps embedded inside XIQ Classic — they have their own left nav that is separate from the main XIQ nav. The hamburger icon (≡) top-left of the micro-app expands the app-specific menu. Without knowing this, you end up navigating the XIQ main menu (Configure, Manage, etc.) and can't find any GE/AD settings. This guide maps both nav trees explicitly before any config steps.

Hour 1 — 0:00 to 1:00

ExtremeGuest Essentials

1A — What Is ExtremeGuest Essentials?

ExtremeGuest Essentials (GE) is a cloud-hosted guest lifecycle management service that wraps an open/CWP SSID with identity, registration, and analytics. It is NOT a replacement for your network policy — it works on top of the AP and SSID you already configured in XIQ.

LayerWho owns itWhat it does
Wireless (RF)AP1 + network policyBroadcasts KhKLab_Guest, handles 802.11 association
Network (VLAN/DHCP)SW1VLAN 10, DHCP 10.10.0.x, IP forwarding
Identity (portal)Guest EssentialsSplash page, registration, expiry, analytics

What GE adds that basic CWP does not

ValidationNoneEmail OTP, SMS OTP, Social login (Facebook/Google/LinkedIn)
Time-limited accessNoneConfigurable expiry (minutes to days)
Guest user recordsNoneFull user database with device, timestamp, source
Sponsor approvalNoneOptional — guest requests, sponsor approves

Key prerequisite you completed yesterday

The CWP post-authentication VLAN override (EP1 → KhKLab_Guest → CWP → post-auth → force VLAN 10) must be set. Without it the guest stays on VLAN 1 even after GE registration completes. This was fixed May 20.

The two items that caused yesterday's confusion

1. Configure → Network — this is where you link KhKLab_Guest to GE. Without this, GE doesn't know which SSID it's serving.

2. Configure → Onboarding → Rules — this creates the SSID + Location → Policy binding. Without a rule, GE receives the guest connection but has no policy to evaluate.

Why the VLAN switches from 1 to 10

Steps 1–5 happen on VLAN 1 (pre-auth). This is intentional — the device needs an IP to reach the splash page. At step 8, AP1 re-associates the client to VLAN 10 because of the post-auth VLAN override you set May 20 (EP1 → KhKLab_Guest → CWP → post-auth → VLAN 10). Without that override, the client stays on VLAN 1 forever even after GE registration completes.

Hour 2 — 1:00 to 2:00

AirDefense Essentials

2A — What Is AirDefense Essentials?

AirDefense Essentials is ExtremeCloud IQ's cloud-based WIDS/WIPS — Wireless Intrusion Detection and Prevention System. Your AP3000 acts as a sensor, scanning RF channels and reporting anomalies to the AirDefense cloud engine. You see results in a dedicated dashboard inside XIQ Classic.

TermMeaning
WIDSWireless Intrusion Detection System — sees threats, raises alarms, takes no action
WIPSWireless Intrusion Prevention System — sees threats AND can contain/terminate rogue devices
SensorAn AP that scans RF channels and reports to AirDefense. AP3000 = Radio-Share sensor.
Radio-ShareAP serves clients on normal channels AND scans for threats simultaneously. Not full-time scanning but sufficient for a lab.
Dedicated SensorAP in monitor-only mode — full-time scanning, no client service. More thorough but sacrifices AP capacity.
Rogue APAn access point not owned by your organization, detected by its BSS MAC falling outside your AP's MAC range
Evil TwinA rogue AP broadcasting the same SSID as your legitimate AP — impersonation attack
Contain / TerminateAirDefense sends deauth frames to clients connected to a rogue AP, forcing disconnection

Step 5 — Optional: adjust Alarm Management

AirDefense app → Settings → Alarm Management

In a home lab, Reconnaissance alarms will fire frequently from neighboring devices (phones, IoT). These are INFO-level and not relevant. You can disable this category to reduce noise while learning:

  • Find Reconnaissance alarm group → click Disable
  • Re-enable later when doing a production deployment

Manual Rogue Sanctioning (mark known device as trusted)

AirDefense → Alarms → select checkbox on Rogue AP alarm → Classify icon → Yes, Sanction

Once sanctioned, the device is added to the sanctioned list and no longer triggers Rogue alarms. Use this for your own neighbor's AP that keeps showing up.

To sync sanctioned alarms to INACTIVE state: Alarms → Sync Rogue Sanctioned Alarm button.

Expected alarm types in a home lab

In a home environment, most alarms will be Info / Minor Reconnaissance from neighbor devices (phones probing for known networks, IoT devices, neighbor APs). You may also see NetBIOS Traffic and ID Theft Sequence — these are common in mixed-device home networks and are not real attacks. A Rogue AP alarm for a neighbor's router is expected and normal — sanction it once identified.
