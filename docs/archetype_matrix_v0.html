§ 00 — How to Read This Document

What this artifact is — and what it is notA regime selector, a parameter taxonomy, and a stadium deep dive. Not a golden set.

The archetype is a regime selector, not a site label. "Dense Public Venue" does not mean "the Lumen Field deployment." It means: any deployment whose propagation signature lands in a specific region of the seven-dimensional regime space — short delay spread, steep path-loss, clustered clients, capacity wall dominant. Lumen is one instance of that class; so is Allegiant Stadium; so is any 72,000-seat bowl with 4 dBm Rician K-factor and 130° angular spread.

The matrix therefore shows two things per archetype: (1) the numeric ranges of the seven regime parameters that define the archetype, and (2) which ~15 of the 60 simulator knobs the optimizer must actually search for that regime — because those are the knobs whose QoE derivative is non-trivially shaped by the regime. Knobs outside the ~15 are either monotone (set them and forget them) or gated off (their subtree is dead for this regime).

Column guide — §03 Stadium Deep-Dive table
  • Effect type — one of the five categories from §02: Mult, Gate, Threshold, Interaction, Context.
  • Current simulator value — what applyOptimal() sets today in the JS model.
  • Why it dominates at stadium — the physical mechanism that promotes this knob to the shortlist for this archetype specifically.
  • Interaction with — the other parameter(s) whose setting changes this knob's QoE derivative. Interaction-coupled knobs must be co-tuned.
  • Optimizer priority — High (search first; biggest potential swing), Medium (refine after highs), Low (trim pass).
Version 0 — Prior elicitation onlyEvery claim in this document is a human expert prior produced before any Morris or Sobol screening has run. The numbers come from the current simulator's embedded responses and from domain reasoning — not from designed experiments against ns-3 or measured field telemetry. This is Tier 0 of the three-tier driver-discovery procedure described in §05 of journey_to_the_digital_twin.html. Version 1 will supersede this document once Morris screening is complete.
§ 01 — Archetypes × Regime

Five Archetypes and Their Seven Regime ParametersClick a row to highlight the corresponding dominant levers in §03.

The seven regime parameters are not user inputs to be typed in — they are a regime estimator. The user selects the archetype that best matches their site; that selection freezes a set of boundary conditions that change the QoE derivative of every one of the 53 active controls. § 02 — Effect-Type Taxonomy

Effect-Type Classification of All 60 ParametersRows 3–5 are the skill zone — the only ones the optimizer must search.

Group parameters by how they act on QoE, not by the UI panel they appear in. The panel grouping (PHY / MAC / SSID) is the right axis for configuration; the effect-type grouping is the right axis for the twin. The classification determines modeling strategy — and reveals that the entire difficulty, and all the golden-set decisions, live in the three bottom categories.

# Effect type Behaviour Skill zone?
1 Multiplicative gain Scales capacity or QoE by a near-fixed factor; composes cleanly across parameters. Direction is known — set and lock. No — capMult chain is correct. Search direction only.
2 Gate / enabler Step function. Off = subtree dead (all child parameters are zero-effect); on = unlocks a regime and reshapes others downstream. No — but turning a gate on changes which other knobs matter.
3 Threshold / non-monotonic skill zone U-shaped response — interior optimum that is bad at both extremes. The optimizer must find the trough. A multiplier chain cannot represent this. Yes — must be searched per archetype. The current simulator hand-places these.
4 Interaction-coupled skill zone Effect on QoE depends on another parameter's current state. Tuning in isolation gives the wrong answer — must be co-tuned with its coupling partner(s). Yes — joint optimization required. Single-parameter sweeps are misleading.
5 Context-modulated skill zone Effect is set entirely by the regime (the seven parameters). The same knob value is correct in one archetype and wrong in another. Per-archetype tuning is mandatory. Yes — must be re-tuned for each regime. Portable golden sets are meaningless for these.

The structural insightThe optimizer searches only the ~15 non-trivial knobs — rows 3–5 of the effect-type taxonomy. Monotone/gate knobs (rows 1–2) are locked to their known-optimal state before the optimizer runs. This is what reduces the 60-D space to a tractable ~12–18-D per archetype — not by ignoring parameters, but by recognizing that most of them have known directions rather than unknown optima.
§ 03 — Stadium Deep Dive

The Dense Public Venue: Dominant-Lever Deep DiveThe ~15 non-trivial knobs for the stadium archetype — the optimizer's search space.

The stadium archetype is the first target because it produces the largest QoE swings, the hardest constraints (POS SLA ≤ 10 ms), and the most acute interactions — every lever that is interesting elsewhere is acute here. The 72,000-seat bowl with 45,000 active WiFi devices at kickoff is the stress-test that exposes non-monotone behavior the flat capMult model cannot represent.

Ignore BC Probe noteIgnore BC Probe (×1.08) ranks first in clean multiplicative effect and is important — but it is already correctly modeled as a row-1 multiplier. The 15 below are the knobs the optimizer must search: the ones where the current simulator hand-placed an interior optimum that the twin must learn to derive.

§ 06 — Governance Note

Version 0 — Prior Elicitation: Governance StatementWhat this document is allowed to claim — and what it is not.

This is a v0 prior: the product of human expert elicitation performed before any data-driven screening has been run. It is the Tier-0 step of the three-tier driver-discovery procedure described in §05 of journey_to_the_digital_twin.html. Every dominant-lever claim in this document carries the epistemological status of hypothesis, not finding.

What v0 is licensed to claim
  • Allowed: "Expert prior — these are the knobs we expect to dominate at stadium based on domain reasoning and simulator inspection."
  • Allowed: "This is the hypothesis the screening procedure (Morris / Sobol) will confirm, modify, or embarrass."
  • Not allowed: "These are the 15 drivers for the Dense Public Venue archetype." That claim requires Morris μ*/σ evidence.
  • Not allowed: "The stadium golden set is [these values]." That claim requires both Morris screening and the optimizer running against the chance-constrained objective — Track A3 in the execution ladder.

Version increment policy

  • v0 → v1: Superseded when Morris screening completes for the Dense Public Venue archetype and produces an evidence-ranked driver list. This document is then archived (not edited) and v1 is the operative reference.
  • v1 → v2: Superseded when Sobol total-order confirmation produces audit-grade first-order and total-order indices. The gap between v1 and v2 ranks is itself a scientific finding.
  • Any version: In-place edits are never made after a version is issued. Each revision is a new file. Past versions are immutable for provenance.
