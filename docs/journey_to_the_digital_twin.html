§ 00 — North Star

The PremiseWhat we are building, and — just as important — what we are not.

We are not building a general-purpose "WiFi industry" digital twin. That object does not exist as a product, and chasing it is a research career, not an SA deliverable. We are building something narrower, more tractable, and more valuable: a parameter-tuning and calibration engine for Extreme AP deployments through Extreme Platform One, whose output is a library of per-archetype golden parameter sets.

The cellular instinct is the right one. In 5G you carry standardized parameter templates for macro, small-cell, dense-urban, indoor-DAS. WiFi has no equivalent — and that absence is not an oversight. Cellular earned its golden sets through enormous measurement campaigns against a scheduled, licensed, deterministic air interface where the operator owns every device. WiFi's medium is contention-based, unlicensed, and full of clients we do not own, so the same parameter that is optimal in one room is wrong two rooms over. No vendor ships golden sets because they cannot be shipped generically — which is exactly why building them, defensibly, is an edge rather than a download.

The unit of the golden-set library is the archetype, not the site. You will never have a set for "Lumen Field." You will have one for the class it belongs to — and Lumen is one instance.

Everything in this record serves that single goal: walk into a site, recognize its archetype, pull a candidate golden set, tune the last ten percent on the ground, and feed what you learned back into the model so the next site of that class starts smarter.

§ 01 — Dimensionality

From a 60-D Nightmare to ~15Why the search space is not 3⁶⁰, and never was.

Sixty parameters with three settings each looks like 4×10²⁸ combinations. It is not, because three forces collapse the effective dimensionality to roughly twelve to eighteen per archetype:

  • Sparsity of effects. A handful of main effects plus a few interactions explain most output variance. The simulator already shows this — Airtime Fairness alone is ×1.38, MU-MIMO streams ×2.8, OFDMA ×1.35. A dozen knobs carry most of the QoE swing; the rest are second-decimal trim.
  • Conditional gating. The 60 form a dependency graph, not a flat cube. MLO Mode, MLO Links, MLO Steering are dead dimensions unless MLO is on and both bands exist. 4K-QAM does nothing unless gen='be' and SNR ≥ 42. Every gate that is off deletes its whole subtree.
  • Monotone saturation. Many knobs are "more is better until it plateaus" — BSS Color, Proxy ARP, Broadcast Filter. Once the direction is known there is nothing to search; you set them on.

But the panel grouping (PHY / MAC / SSID) is the wrong axis for the twin. Group instead by how each parameter acts on QoE, because that dictates how it must be modeled — and reveals that the entire difficulty lives in three of five categories.

The structural insightThe current simulator models everything as rows 1–2 (multipliers and switches). That is why it is an honest teaching tool but a pre-alpha tuning engine: the real golden-set decisions are all made in rows 3–5, which a chain of m *= factor cannot represent. A multiplier chain can never derive an interior optimum — someone hand-placed TX=17, GI=1600, MaxClients=100. The twin's job is to make those optima emerge.
§ 02 — The Regime

The Seven Regime ParametersNot user inputs. A regime selector that changes what the 53 controls do.

The seven are not numbers a user measures and types in. They are a regime selector — a dropdown where the user estimates "this is a warehouse" or "this is a stadium bowl." That estimate freezes a set of boundary conditions, and those boundary conditions change the QoE derivative of every one of the 53 controls. Guard Interval at 800 ns is correct in a clinic and wrong in a warehouse; the parameter did not change — the regime did.

What physically defines an archetype is its propagation signature, not its name. "Dense venue" versus "warehouse" is really "short delay spread, clustered clients, steep capacity wall" versus "long delay spread, dispersed clients, coverage wall." Eventually Sionna measures these from a survey; until then, the user estimates the bucket.

§ 03 — The Dependent Variable

The Stadium QoE ObjectiveBefore optimizing anything, write down what "good" means — explicitly, and defensibly.

QoE is a vector, not a scalar, and parameters trade its components against one another. There is therefore no single optimum — there is a Pareto frontier, and the archetype's SLA priority order selects a point on it. Stadium, mall, and convention center share the same frontier but pick different points, because their objective functions have different shapes. This is the most fundamental dependent-variable choice in the whole project, and the first thing an auditor attacks: "why are you maximizing that?"

Objective — Dense Public Venue (Stadium)

Maximize proportional-fairness utility over admitted clients:

U(θ) = Σi∈admitted log( throughputi(θ) )

Subject to hard constraints (satisfied, not maximized):

  • POS / payment latency ≤ 10 ms (≤ 3 ms where rTWT is on) — VLAN 50
  • Per-class throughput floors met: VIP ≥ 25 Mbps, Teams/Video ≥ 5 Mbps
  • Packet loss ≤ 1% on latency-sensitive classes
  • Admitted-client count ≥ deployment target (cannot strand fans)

Priority order on conflict:  POS SLA > fairness > admitted count > aggregate throughput > per-client peak

Explicitly sacrificed: peak per-client throughput for strong clients. In a 72k bowl full of slow phones, fairness-or-collapse dominates raw speed.

Why proportional fairnessThe Σ log(throughput) form is the standard, literature-grounded utility that automatically balances aggregate throughput against equity — maximizing it is mathematically equivalent to proportional fairness. It encodes the stadium's exact weighting without a hand-tuned fairness coefficient, which makes it far easier to defend than "we picked weights that looked right."
Robust form — evaluated under Monte Carlo (Track A optimizer)

The point objective above is what the optimizer maximizes deterministically. Wrapped in Monte Carlo over demand, it becomes a chance-constrained robustness objective:

maximize P50[ U(θ) ] − k · ( P95[U] − P5[U] ) subject to P( POS_latency ≤ 10ms ) ≥ 99%

Central tendency minus a spread penalty, with the POS SLA enforced as a chance constraint rather than a soft penalty. This is the objective that makes the optimizer prefer a config that holds up at P95 kickoff surge over one that wins at P50 but breaches under load — the extra thing Monte Carlo buys (§07, Track A).

How mall and convention differ

  • Mall — diffuse, roaming-heavy, long dwell. Objective weights coverage continuity + roaming smoothness and the POS SLA; raw capacity is secondary. The utility adds a roaming-disruption penalty and a coverage-hole term.
  • Convention center — bursty, reconfigurable, uplink-heavy (everyone uploading). Objective weights uplink capacity + rapid spatial load rebalancing. Almost a mobile stadium.

Same parameter space, same frontier — different chosen point. That weighting, not the parameter values, is what an archetype actually is.

§ 04 — Two Verbs

Optimization vs CalibrationThe distinction that makes or breaks the evidence trail.

OptimizationCalibration
Held fixedThe modelReality (measurements)
AdjustedThe parametersThe model itself
Question"Which settings maximize QoE in this model?""Why does the model's QoE differ from what AP2 measured?"
Ground truthns-3 / the surrogateLab / field telemetry
Enters atNow (ladder steps 2–4)Later (ladder step 7)
The trap to avoidTuning the 15 in ns-3 to push QoE up is optimization — ns-3 is the synthetic ground truth at that stage; there is nothing to calibrate against until real measurements arrive. Using "calibrate" for both confuses the operator, the agents' prompts, and — critically — the auditor, because the evidence for "we found good settings" is entirely different from the evidence for "our model matches reality." Two verbs, two boxes, two evidence trails, from day one.
§ 05 — Driver Discovery

Finding the ~15We do not pick the drivers. A ranking procedure picks them — and the procedure is the thing we defend.

The discovery runs in three escalating tiers, cheapest first, and is run once per archetype with the seven regime parameters frozen at that archetype's ranges. The output is not "the 15 drivers" — it is five ranked lists, and the differences between them are the scientific finding.

Tier 0 — Prior elicitation

Before any compute: you (and ideally two other SAs) independently rank which knobs you expect to matter per archetype, each with a one-line physical rationale. This is not the answer — it is the prior, and it has two jobs: it is the hypothesis the data will confirm or embarrass, and it is the baseline an auditor compares the data-driven result against. Disagreement between prior and data is itself evidence.

Tier 1 — Morris elementary effects

A few hundred designed runs produce, per parameter, two numbers: μ* (mean absolute effect — how much it moves QoE) and σ (how much that effect varies with other parameters' settings). The μ*–σ scatter is the screening verdict:

μ*σVerdictModeling consequence
lowlowNegligibleDrop it — set a sane default, stop tuning
highlowClean main effectA multiplier — the capMult chain is correct for it
highhighInteraction / non-monotoneA driver — must be co-tuned, never in isolation

The ~15 drivers per archetype are everything that is not in the bottom-left corner.

Tier 2 — Sobol total-order confirmation

Run on the surrogate (§07–08), Sobol quantifies each parameter's first-order index (its solo contribution to QoE variance) and total-order index (including every interaction it participates in). The gap between total and first-order is a direct measurement of how interaction-coupled a parameter is — the rigorous, automated version of the hand-discovery of TXOP-BE × rTWT. Morris screens; Sobol is the audit-grade evidence.

If two archetypes produce nearly identical driver rankings, you have learned they should be merged. The screening procedure validates the taxonomy as a side effect.
§ 06 — Governance

Governance, in Two RegistersThe selection of driver variables is itself a decision — and decisions must be auditable and self-correcting.

What we claim to an auditor is not that the fifteen are correct. It is that the process that selected them is sound, documented, and self-correcting. "We picked these because they seemed important" is not auditable. The two panels below are the same discipline at two levels of formality — pick the tab for the audience.

Internal — for the operator
External — for the auditor

Recalibration triggers

  • Field prediction error exceeds threshold on a deployed golden set → re-fit values.
  • Field data re-ranks a driver beyond a set tolerance → flag a driver re-ranking event (not a silent overwrite).
  • Two archetypes' rankings converge → propose a merge.

Lives alongside the git-committed EOD blueprints — same provenance discipline, applied to the variable-selection decision rather than the config.

Purpose · defensibility to an independent reviewer

For a customer, regulator, or Extreme internal review. Heavier on method statement, provenance, and falsification.

1 · Methodology statement

Driver variables are selected by a pre-registered three-tier procedure — prior elicitation, Morris screening, Sobol confirmation — run independently per environment archetype. No variable is included or excluded without recorded quantitative evidence. The procedure, not the selection, is the object of the claim.

2 · Provenance & versioning policy

Every driver list is stamped with the archetype-definition version, screening run identifier, data-source hash (ns-3 configuration or telemetry window), and the surrogate version it derives from. Any change is a version increment, never an in-place edit, so any past claim is reproducible.

3 · Challenge protocol (pre-registered falsification)

For each archetype we record, in advance, the field observation that would falsify the current driver list — answering the auditor's first question, "what evidence would change your mind?", before it is asked.

Example — Warehouse"If warehouse deployments show airtime fairness mattering more than coverage thresholds, the warehouse driver ranking is wrong and must be re-screened." A pre-registered falsifier per archetype.

4 · Change control & sign-off

A driver re-ranking triggered by field data is a controlled event: it is logged, requires named sign-off, and carries the evidence that justified it. Value re-fits (optimization) and model re-fits (calibration) are recorded on separate trails and never conflated.

5 · Separation of evidence

Claims of the form "these settings perform well" (optimization) are supported by model/search evidence; claims of the form "the model reflects reality" (calibration) are supported by measurement evidence. The two are never used to support each other.

Track A — near-term, on the existing model SHIPS NOW

  1. A1
    Archetype matrix HTML START HEREStadium-focused; the seven regime parameters and dominant levers per archetype. Standalone strategic artifact, no simulator dependency.
  2. A2
    Monte Carlo tabPoisson client arrivals + Pareto per-client demand → P5/P50/P95 bands and breach probability. Robustness display + NOC forward estimates. Label the bands as model uncertainty, not measurement uncertainty — the coefficients are still unvalidated priors.
  3. A3
    Optimizer tabSearch the ~15 non-trivial knobs (lock the monotone/gate knobs to optimal per §01); maximize the chance-constrained stadium objective (§03). This is where Monte Carlo + optimizer combine — and where the result can differ from hand-coded "Apply Optimal," specifically around the threshold and interaction knobs.
Why the optimizer needs Monte Carlo here — the sharpened pointOn a strictly multiplicative, monotone model, Monte Carlo only adds bands; the optimum does not move. But the current model is not strictly monotone — it already contains a load-threshold (cwBe=7 & u>0.7 → +3ms), an interaction (TXOP-BE × rTWT → POS latency), and a hard SLA (POS ≤ 10 ms). Under stochastic demand, some draws cross the airtime cliff; a chance-constrained optimizer therefore prefers configs that keep P(breach) low — a different answer than the point optimizer, precisely at the rows-3–4 knobs that later become the driver-discovery targets. So "I want both" is not aesthetic; it is technically necessary the moment a threshold or constraint exists.

Track B — medium-term · the ns-3 / Sionna ladder DEFERRED

Sionna is the RF/physics engine: given a 3D room and antennas, it ray-traces propagation and outputs the channel — the SNR and multipath each client sees, and the very seven regime parameters. It answers "what does the air look like here?" ns-3 is the protocol engine: given those channels plus the 60 knobs, it simulates the real 802.11 MAC — contention, OFDMA, retries, airtime, MLO — and emits the QoE vector. It answers "given that air and these settings, what experience results?" Sionna feeds ns-3. They are the high-fidelity ground truth the fast surrogate trains against; they are not run per-deployment. Starter files (stadium-wifi.cc, qoe_harness.py) are written and parked for when Track B begins.

  1. 1
    ns-3 alone, stock channel1 AP, N STAs, 802.11ax, FlowMonitor. Reproduce one row of the simulator (e.g. AF on/off), confirm direction. Learn the WiFi module in isolation. Begins when Track A's optimizer needs a higher-fidelity engine to optimize against.
  2. 2
    ns-3 screening harnessWrap ns-3 in Python; run a Morris design over ~15 candidate knobs for the stadium archetype. First data-driven driver ranking vs the Tier-0 prior.
  3. 3
    Surrogate fitTrain a gradient-boosted-tree surrogate on the ns-3 runs. A fast twin for one archetype, plus feature importances to cross-check Morris.
  4. 4
    Sobol on the surrogateQuantify first-order vs total. Lock the audit-grade driver list for archetype #1.
  5. 5
    Sionna, one roomModel a lab room, generate channels, confirm the seven come out in range. Where "user estimates the archetype" later becomes "Sionna measures it."
  6. 6
    Couple themFeed Sionna channels into ns-3 — full high-fidelity twin for one archetype. Repeat 2–6 per archetype.
  7. 7
    Calibrate to the lab APRun a candidate golden set on AP2/SW2, measure via the EP1/XIQ API, compare to prediction, feed the error back. The loop closes — this is where it becomes a twin.
Honest caveatns-3's EDCA/airtime model is standards-compliant 802.11 — it is not a literal model of Extreme's "Airtime Fairness: Strict," which is a vendor scheduler on top. Early ns-3 QoE tells you directionally whether a lever helps and how the physics responds; it is not yet a faithful twin of an Extreme AP. That faithfulness is exactly what step-7 calibration buys.

§ 09 — The Roadmap

The Fidelity LadderEach rung adds one capability the rung below structurally cannot express.

  1. 1
    Independent multiplicative model YOU ARE HEREEach knob a fixed factor. Honest for effect-types 1–2. The current simulator.
  2. 2
    + Non-monotonic responsesReplace scalars with curves for threshold knobs (RX-SOP, CWmin, TX, Min-RSSI). Interior optima emerge instead of being hand-set.
  3. 3
    + Interaction termsAdd the two-way couplings Sobol surfaces. TXOP × rTWT is in the math, not a footnote.
  4. 4
    + Context modulation by the sevenThe same surrogate, coefficients now functions of the regime. One model, five behaviours.
  5. 5
    + Multi-objective ParetoStop collapsing to one 0–100 score; expose the trade-off front; let SLA priority pick the point.
  6. 6
    + Calibration to ground truthCoefficients fit to ns-3 / telemetry, with the feedback loop. This is what turns "simulator" into "twin."

The jump from rung 1 to rung 6 is not a rewrite. The UI, Gap Analysis, Breaking-Point Finder, and Config Export all survive intact. What changes is the engine under capMult: scalars become calibrated functions, and a sensitivity stage tells you which scalars were lies.

Immediate target · Track AThe near-term work reaches up the ladder without ns-3: the Monte Carlo + optimizer tabs deliver a partial rung 5 (multi-objective / robustness, via the chance-constrained objective) and motivate rung 2 (the threshold knobs whose curves Monte Carlo proves matter).
