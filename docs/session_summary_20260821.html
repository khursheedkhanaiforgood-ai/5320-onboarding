Root Cause — Identified, Not Yet Fixed

Core Conflict — Native VLAN vs. Tagged Client VLAN Auto-sense/FA assigns VLAN 10 as the native (untagged) VLAN on port 1/3. This is by design — the AP management uses VLAN 10 untagged.

EP1 User Profile assigns Staff clients to VLAN 10, so the AP sends client frames tagged VLAN 10 over port 1/3.

Tagged VLAN 10 frames arriving on a port where VLAN 10 is native are dropped. The DHCP Discover never reaches the switch DHCP server.
Why FA Would Fix This When FA negotiates properly, the AP tells the switch which I-SIDs it needs for client traffic. The switch then adds VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 as tagged members on port 1/3, separate from the native management VLAN. FA is not negotiating — show fa agent shows no clients. Without FA, only VLAN 10 native exists on port 1/3 and tagged client traffic cannot flow.

Secondary Suspect — DHCP HA Status: Initialising show ip dhcp-server shows HA Status: Initialising with no HA peer (0.0.0.0). It is unknown whether this state prevents the server from responding to DHCP Discovers even though Operational HA role = primary. Karl to confirm VOSS 9.4 behavior.

1 Why is FA not negotiating? show fa agent shows no clients even though AP is online and port 1/3 is in VLAN 10. FA negotiation is the proper fix — it would add tagged VLANs 10/20 to port 1/3 separately from the native management VLAN.
2 Does HA Status: Initialising (no HA peer) prevent the DHCP server from serving leases in VOSS 9.4? If yes, this is a second blocker even if the VLAN conflict is resolved. May need ip dhcp-server ha-role standalone or similar — verify syntax before running.
3 In a wap-type1 FA design, how do Staff clients (PPSK → VLAN 10) get DHCP when VLAN 10 is also the AP management native VLAN on port 1/3? The fundamental design question. Is a separate AP management I-SID required? What is the correct I-SID/VLAN split for PPSK with two client VLANs?
4 What brings Vlan20 UP? FA needs to add VLAN 20 as tagged to port 1/3 for Guest to work. Vlan20 SVI stays DOWN until at least one port joins VLAN 20. FA should handle this — but FA is not working.
5 Why is SW2 offline on EP1 management plane? IQAgent may not be running or registered. Lower priority — AP traffic does not depend on EP1 seeing the switch. But worth fixing so Karl can push config via XIQ.
